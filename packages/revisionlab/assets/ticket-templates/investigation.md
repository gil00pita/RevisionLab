# Investigation

Organize the description with these Markdown headings:
## Observations
Summarize feedback and observed test activity without treating metrics as proof of a defect.
## Questions to answer
Identify what the team still needs to learn.
## Investigation plan
Suggest focused review or testing grounded in the sources.
## Evidence
Reference the relevant comments, screenshots, and test sessions.

Write acceptanceCriteria as a checklist of investigation outcomes and decisions. Do not promise implementation or fabricate participant quotes.
